French matches

Offers Ciqual entries for the foods Livsmedelsverket and Frida have none for. Runs on your device — nothing is uploaded.

1Get the table

From ciqual.anses.fr, the full table as Excel. Built against the 2025 English release.

Take the English export, not the French one. Both sides speaking the same language is the whole advantage this audit has over the Swedish one: a candidate that disagrees about fresh or dried gets pushed down instead of being left for you to catch.

Ciqual gives no confidence code per figure, so there is no per-value test here as there is for the American set. It is taken on the table's standing, the way Sweden and Denmark are — which is why France sits below Denmark on the ladder.

3Pick a match for each food

The top line is often the wrong form of the right food — that is how every mismatch so far has looked, and this table is no kinder: it will offer you plain yoghurt for labneh, sesame seed for halva and raw lemon for a preserved one. The figures under each candidate are there to catch it: a dried food with 80g of water is not the dried one. Tap to pick, tap again to unpick. If the right entry is not among the three, search for it — the box reaches every food in the file.
