Harvest

Starts from the table rather than from a food. Runs on your device — nothing is uploaded.

1What this is for

The other tools ask does this food we chose have figures? This one asks which of these figures is worth a food? Feed it any of the three exports and it drops everything incomplete, everything already matched, and everything unremarkable — and hands back what is left, most extreme first.

It does not decide. It cannot tell a food from a prepared dish, and it has no idea whether anyone buys the thing. Both of those are yours. What it does is make three thousand rows into a list you can actually read.

The ladder does not apply here. Livsmedelsverket, Frida and Ciqual are all laboratory analyses, and the ladder does not claim otherwise — it exists to settle which figure to take when two tables answer for the same food, on grounds of consistency and of which country's shelf the record describes. Neither applies to choosing what to list. So a record is dropped here only when some source already answers for that food, whichever source it is, and what is left is sorted on its figures rather than on where it came from.

A record has to carry fat, protein, carbohydrate, fibre, sugars and water to get through at all — stricter than the backbone the audits require, because there is no reason to spend attention on a record that arrives already short. Alcohol is not required: most tables leave the column out for anything that is not a drink, and its absence there means zero.

4Take the picks away

Everything ticked above, with its figures, as tools/harvest-picks.json. Send it over and it becomes foods — a name, a category, a portion and a trait list are decisions, and they are not made here.

Then build. Once the foods land in the repo they are names with no figures until build-nutrition.html has run — the Livsmedelsverket export in step 2, nothing in step 3. It is the step that feels unnecessary because the picking is what took the effort, and it is the one that puts the figures where the site reads them.

Picks are kept in this browser as you go, so reloading the page or coming back tomorrow does not lose them.
