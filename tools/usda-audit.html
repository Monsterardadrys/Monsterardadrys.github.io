American matches

Offers USDA entries for the foods Livsmedelsverket, Frida and Ciqual have none for. Runs on your device — the file never leaves it.

1Get the dataset

From fdc.nal.usda.gov, as JSON. Two of the four sets are read here:

  • SR Legacy — 7,800-odd foods and the reason to be here. It is where the spices, the sauces and the dried fruit live.
  • Foundation Foods — 363 foods, mostly raw produce that Livsmedelsverket already covers. Small and clean; little left to gain.

FNDDS is not read. It is survey data costed out of recipes rather than measured, which is the same objection that keeps the Branded Foods set off the ladder entirely. Feeding it in here will produce nothing.

2Open the file here

A 64MB file takes a few seconds and is read a piece at a time, so the page stays under a phone's memory rather than loading the lot.

3Pick a match for each food

The top line is often the wrong form of the right food — that is how every mismatch so far has looked, and a plain word-match on this set pairs "Dried Lychee" with "Figs, dried" on the word dried. The figures under each candidate are there to catch it: a dried food with 80g of water is not the dried one. Tap to pick, tap again to unpick. If the right entry is not among the three, search for it — the box reaches every food in the file.

4Take the picks away

Everything picked above, as the whole of tools/usda-aliases.json. Download it, or copy the lot in one go — no need to transcribe anything.

Picks are kept in this browser as you go, so reloading the page or coming back tomorrow does not lose them.

And the figures

Built from the picks directly, so there is no second pass. Put it in the project root as nutrition-usda.js. Livsmedelsverket and Frida both still win wherever they have a figure.
