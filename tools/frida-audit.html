Danish matches

Offers Frida entries for the foods Livsmedelsverket has none for. Runs on your device — nothing is uploaded.

1Get the dataset

Open frida.fooddata.dk and download the full dataset as Excel. Version 6.1 is what this page was built against.

Frida names every food in English as well as Danish, so there is no translation step here — unlike the Swedish audit, which needs one.

2Open the file here

3Pick a match for each food

The top line is often the wrong form of the right food — that is how every mismatch so far has looked. The figures under each candidate are there to catch it: a dried food with 80g of water is not the dried one. Tap to pick, tap again to unpick. If the right entry is not among the three, search for it — the box reaches every food in the file.

0 picked — go to the file ↓

4Take the picks away

Everything picked above, as the whole of tools/frida-aliases.json. Download it, or copy the lot in one go — no need to transcribe anything.

Picks are kept in this browser as you go, so reloading the page or coming back tomorrow does not lose them.

And the figures

Built from the picks directly, so there is no second pass. Put it in the project root as nutrition-frida.js. Livsmedelsverket still wins wherever both have a figure.
