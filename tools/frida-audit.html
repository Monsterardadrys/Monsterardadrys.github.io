Danish matches

Offers Frida entries for the foods Livsmedelsverket has none for. Runs on your device — nothing is uploaded.

1Get the dataset

Open frida.fooddata.dk and download the full dataset as Excel. Version 6.1 is what this page was built against.

Frida names every food in English as well as Danish, so there is no translation step here — unlike the Swedish audit, which needs one.

2Open the file here

3Read all three candidates

The top line is often the wrong form of the right food — that is how every mismatch so far has looked. The figures under each candidate are there to catch it: a dried food with 80g of water is not the dried one. Tap a candidate to copy its line for frida-aliases.json.

4Download the figures

Once pairs are confirmed in tools/frida-aliases.json, reload this page with the same file and download nutrition-frida.js into the project root. Livsmedelsverket still wins wherever both have a figure.
