Food intolerance guide

    A database of the food properties that can cause discomfort.

    FODMAPs, histamine, the fourteen declarable allergens, salicylates, bile stimulants — which foods carry them, in what amount, and on what evidence. Built by a dietitian for dietitians and other health professions, as continuing-education material.

    Mark several foods and the tool lists what they have in common. It is a lookup: it describes foods, not people, and it diagnoses nothing.

      This is a free sample

      The sample has no time limit, needs no account and sends nothing anywhere. It is limited in size, not in time.

      • 100 of 582 foods with properties and nutrition figures. The rest appear in the lists, greyed out.
      • 30 of 43 properties. All fourteen declarable allergens are here. What is missing is the detail below them: the FODMAP types separately, the pollen cross-reactions, and the individual irritant mechanisms.
      • Two of three tools. The third asks the question the other way round: which foods carry none of a given set of properties?
      • Whole: both languages, every article, the method page and the sources.

      The finished app arrives in 2027. Until then it keeps being built, and this site keeps up with it.
