Where the numbers come from

Livsmedelsverket's food database — the Swedish Food Agency's national food composition database, roughly 2 600 foods with over 50 nutrients each — is the main reference for fat, protein, fiber, sugar and alcohol content in this tool. Every food below that is marked as checked has had its threshold-based traits compared against that database entry by entry.

Not every food is in it. Cheeses like Roquefort and Fontina, spice blends like za'atar, and most Asian sauces have no Swedish entry, so their figures come from published nutrition data and clinical literature instead. Those foods are listed here too, marked as such — the point of this page is that you can tell which is which.

FODMAP subtypes come from the Monash University database rather than Livsmedelsverket, and allergens, cross-reactivity and GI irritant mechanisms come from clinical literature. Those are not what this page tracks. See How the analysis works for the full picture, and How Certain Is Any of This? for where the uncertainty sits.
